# Archived particle portrait experiment

This asset is no longer used by the site. The current hero uses a procedural fibre-optic cable. The source image and prompt are retained for recovery.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. This is a fictional decorative woman, not a likeness of the portfolio owner. The live canvas samples its alpha, color and luminance; it does not draw the photo on top of the particles. Cursor repulsion and scroll morphing apply to those sampled points.

Saved asset: `public/images/portrait-natural-v3.png`.

Final generation prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: source portrait for an interactive particle sculpture on a professional portfolio. Generate one original fictional adult woman, not a likeness of any real person. A naturally proportioned head-and-shoulders portrait down to mid chest, looking straight at the camera, both eyes visible, relaxed brows and a subtle friendly closed-mouth smile. Dark softly waved hair framing both sides of her face, understated dark tailored jacket over a light top. Natural warm skin tones, lifelike eyes with gentle catchlights, realistic cheeks and nose, real skin texture. Soft directional studio light with generous fill so the face has readable natural tonal depth and no hollow or black eye sockets. Eye-level 85mm editorial photography, centered portrait, entire head and both shoulders visible with a little padding. Genuinely transparent background with clean alpha edges. This is a normal continuous-tone photograph, NOT a dot picture; software will convert it into dots later. No text, no logos, no jewelry emphasis, no ghostly pallor, no doll, no mask, no horror mood, no overly symmetrical artificial face, no harsh outlines.
